# Salaryman idle generation prompts

Creation route: generated-draft-refine. The generated design and pixel-art draft were imported and refined in native Pixelorama; the final source has a generated/refined base and a native blink layer.

## Character design reference

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one full-body pixel-art character design reference for PixWeft's README showcase, to guide later native Pixelorama drawing and idle animation.
Primary request: design a memorable, appealing contemporary salaryman, an exhausted adult office worker in his thirties, quietly standing in place. ONE character and ONE pose only.
Subject: slightly tousled short dark hair, expressive weary half-lidded eyes and subtly raised brow, modest under-eye shadows, a small resigned smile; an attractive readable face with character rather than ugliness or grotesque caricature. A slightly rumpled pale blue collared shirt with rolled sleeves, loosened muted brick-red necktie, charcoal tailored slacks, visible belt, neat worn leather work shoes, and an ID card on a thin lanyard. A single small takeaway coffee cup held naturally in one hand; the other arm rests loosely at his side. Hands, fingers, both shoes, hair, tie and lanyard fully visible and anatomically coherent.
Style/medium: exceptionally polished contemporary comic pixel art, like a charming protagonist in an expressive indie adventure game. True deliberate square pixel clusters, stepped contours, selective dark outlines, crisp blocked fabric folds and sculpted face planes; no smooth airbrushed edges, no vector geometric placeholder body, no blurred downsampling, no painterly noise. Design around a 96-by-128 logical pixel canvas, presented enlarged with crisp nearest-neighbor-like pixel blocks. Prioritize beautiful authored shape design and readable costume details over tiny texture.
Composition/framing: front three-quarter view, facing slightly toward the viewer's left; entire body from hair to shoe soles inside the image, centered with generous transparent padding on every side. Relaxed balanced standing stance with slightly lowered shoulders, separated feet, clear negative spaces around the arms, cup and legs. Suitable as the baseline for a future small breathing bob and blink loop, but do not show multiple poses or motion ghosts.
Lighting/mood: tasteful warm highlights and cool blue-grey clothing shadows, clear material separation, gentle tired-office-worker humor and human warmth. Rich harmonious colors without a numerical palette restriction.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background. No environment, floor, backdrop pattern, ground shadow, text, letters, logo, watermark, UI, sheet grid, labels, extra characters, or extra props.
Deliverable: a single excellent full-body character design reference, not a sprite sheet and not an animation.

```

## Pixel-art draft

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE pixel-art game character draft for native Pixelorama inspection, local refinement, and an eight-frame breathing/blinking idle animation.
Input image: salaryman-design-v2.png is the approved identity, outfit, expression, hand/cup grip, and overall design reference. Preserve this charming tired office-worker man, not a generic replacement.
Primary request: translate the approved full-body office worker into a beautiful genuinely low-resolution pixel-art adventure-game sprite. ONE character, ONE still standing pose; not a sheet.
Subject: adult tired salaryman with short tousled dark hair, expressive weary half-lidded face and small resigned smile, pale blue rolled-sleeve collared shirt, loosened muted red tie, ID lanyard and badge, dark charcoal trousers and brown leather shoes. One takeaway coffee cup in his hand. Keep both complete hands, cup, tie, badge, arms, legs, and shoes clearly readable.
Pixel technique: design on an actual 160-by-160 logical square canvas with deliberate chunky square clusters and clean stepped edges, displayed enlarged using crisp pixel blocks. The head should have enough pixels for expressive eyes and eyebrows, around 25 to 30 logical pixels high. Human proportions may be gently adapted to roughly five heads tall for game readability, while preserving the reference's identity and appealing expression. Each fold, hair tuft, finger group and shoe plane must resolve into readable pixel shapes, not thin noisy illustration lines. Thoughtful colors and material shading, no numerical palette restriction. Selective dark outlines, crisp highlights, no airbrush, gradients, blur, dithering, photorealism, smooth vector curves or geometric placeholder figure.
Composition: centered full body from hair tip through both shoe soles, facing the same front three-quarter direction as the reference. Relaxed standing weight, both feet solidly planted on the same invisible ground plane. Keep clear transparent margin on all four sides, with the figure approximately 140 logical pixels tall. Hands and coffee cup completely inside the canvas. Empty transparent background and no ground shadow.
Delivery: one outstanding square sprite draft suitable for preserving its design while doing native pixel-level corrections and subtle upper-body breathing/blink edits. No animation, multiple poses, text, labels, logos, additional props, scene or backdrop.

```
